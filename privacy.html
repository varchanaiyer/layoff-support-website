Privacy policy

Your privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026. Applies to the Layoff Support app and this website.

Layoff Support is run by Data and AI Consulting PTE LTD in Singapore ("we"). We built it for people going through one of the hardest weeks of their working life, so we collect as little as we can.

The Layoff Support app

  • Everything you enter stays on your phone. Your dates, answers, notes and progress are saved only on your device. We don't run accounts, and the app does not send any of this to us or anyone else.
  • No tracking or ads. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • Reminders are local. If you turn on nudges, they are scheduled by your phone. No notification content passes through our servers.
  • Links open other sites. When you tap a link to a helpline, government site or service, that site's own privacy policy applies.
  • Deleting your data. Use "Delete everything on this phone" in the About you screen, or delete the app. Either removes everything it stored.

This website

  • Your plan (deadlines and coverage) is worked out in your browser and saved only in your browser's local storage. It is not sent to us.
  • Forms. If you sign up for the marketplace or send us a message, we receive what you type (such as your name, email, the work you do and your notes) through our form provider, Formspark. We use it only to reply to you and to tell you about the marketplace. You can ask us to delete it at any time.
  • Fonts. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers.
  • We don't use advertising cookies or sell personal information.

Who else sees your information

Only the service providers that run the website and forms for us (our host and Formspark), and only to provide that service. We don't sell or share your information for marketing.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. We'll answer within 30 days.

Children

Layoff Support is meant for adults and isn't directed at children under 16.

Not professional advice

Layoff Support shares general information to help you find your way. It is not legal, medical or financial advice. Please check important decisions with a professional or the official source we link to.
