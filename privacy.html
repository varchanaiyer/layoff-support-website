Privacy policy

Your privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026. Applies to the Layoff Support app and this website.

Layoff Support is run by Data and AI Consulting PTE LTD in Singapore ("we"). We built it for people going through one of the hardest weeks of their working life, so we collect as little as we can.

The Layoff Support app

  • Everything you enter stays on your phone. Your dates, answers, notes and progress are saved only on your device. We don't run accounts, and the app does not send any of this to us or anyone else.
  • No tracking or ads. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • Reminders are local. If you turn on nudges, they are scheduled by your phone. No notification content passes through our servers.
  • Links open other sites. When you tap a link to a helpline, government site or service, that site's own privacy policy applies.
  • Deleting your data. Use "Delete everything on this phone" in the About you screen, or delete the app. Either removes everything it stored.

This website

  • Your plan (deadlines and coverage) is worked out in your browser. If you're not signed in, it's saved only in your browser and not sent to us.
  • Forms. If you send us a message, we receive what you type (such as your name, email and message) through our form provider, Formspark. We use it only to reply to you. You can ask us to delete it at any time.
  • Fonts. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers.
  • We don't use advertising cookies or sell personal information.

Website accounts

  • What we store. If you create a free account, we store your email address and, if you sign in with Google, your name. We also store what you choose to save: your plan and deadlines, your checklist progress, and anything you add on your progress page (such as your state, your field, your last day of work, what you need help with, and circles or pods you asked to join).
  • Why. To keep your progress between visits, to show options that fit your situation, and to email you sign-in links. We only send reminders and help that fits your situation if you turn that on, and every such email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Who can see it. Only you, and us. Accounts are run by our provider Supabase, and each person can only read their own data.
  • Members of our earlier site. If you had an account on our previous website, we kept your name, email and the date you joined so we can recognise you when you sign in here.
  • Deleting it. Use "Delete my data" on your progress page, or ask us to close your account.

Who else sees your information

Only the service providers that run the website, accounts and forms for us (our host GitHub, Supabase and Formspark), and only to provide that service. We don't sell or share your information for marketing.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. We'll answer within 30 days.

Children

Layoff Support is meant for adults and isn't directed at children under 16.

Not professional advice

Layoff Support shares general information to help you find your way. It is not legal, medical or financial advice. Please check important decisions with a professional or the official source we link to.
