What to look for in a therapist right now

Things our community wishes they'd known before their first session.

  • Ask about layoff and grief experience. Career loss is an identity loss. Not all therapists frame it that way.
  • Sliding scale is normal. It's okay to ask. Many practitioners hold a few low-cost slots and won't mention them unless you do.
  • COBRA may keep your benefits. If you're in the US, your old employer's plan can often continue for 18 months — sometimes subsidized.
  • Don't optimize, just start. The first therapist doesn't need to be the perfect fit. Just one human, this week, beats waiting three.
