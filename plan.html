Rough estimate for planning only. It uses 2026 federal poverty guidelines and a typical benchmark plan. Your real price comes from HealthCare.gov or your state marketplace.

New for 2026The extra marketplace subsidies ended on December 31, 2025, and there's no longer a cap on paying back excess subsidy at tax time. If a lump-sum severance or a quick new job pushes your income up, update your marketplace income right away.
The COBRA safety netYou have 60 days to elect COBRA, and it covers you back to day one once you pay. Many people pick a marketplace plan and keep COBRA as a backup in case something happens while they decide.
