Asking for a better severance package

Hi [name], Thank you for the severance offer. Before I sign, I'd like to ask for a few changes, given [my X years with the company / the role I played in Y / how close I am to my bonus or vesting date]: - [Severance pay: e.g. 12 weeks instead of 8] - [Health coverage: the company paying COBRA premiums for the same period] - [Equity: accelerated vesting of shares due before (date)] - [A neutral reference and agreed wording for the departure] I'd also like until [date] to review the agreement. Happy to talk this week. Thanks, [Your name]
