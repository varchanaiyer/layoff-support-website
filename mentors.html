We're building our mentor network

It's just starting, and we'd rather tell you that than show you a wall of profiles. Here's how it will work:

  1. Tell us what would help. Your resume, interview practice, changing careers, severance, or just someone to listen.
  2. We introduce you to one mentor by email, someone who's been where you are or who hires for the kind of role you want.
  3. A free 15-minute video call. No pitch, nothing to buy.

Need someone to talk to today?

You don't have to wait for us. These are free right now:

  • ADPList: book a 1:1 video call with a mentor, including hiring managers and recruiters. Many sessions are free.
  • 7 Cups: talk any time with a trained volunteer listener.
  • Free online groups: people going through the same thing, meeting every week. You can keep your camera off.
