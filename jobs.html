What to look for

  • Pay band or skip it. If a listing won't show pay, you'll be negotiating in the dark against someone who does this every day.
  • Read the interview process before the JD. Four rounds plus a take-home is a part-time job you're doing for free.
  • A "gap" is not a red flag to anyone worth working for. If a recruiter flinches at the word "layoff," note it and move on.
