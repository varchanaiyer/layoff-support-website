Ask for free — the script

Most paid tools don't advertise hardship pricing on their site. Many will quietly say yes if you ask. Copy this, paste it into the support inbox of any tool you're paying for, change the name, and send it.

Subject: Hardship request — recently laid off

Hi — I've been a paying user since [date]. I was laid off [last week / last month] and I'm trying to keep the tools that are actually useful while I look for the next role. Do you have any kind of unemployed, hardship, or pause option? I'd rather ask than cancel. Thank you for considering it. — [Name]

It works more often than you'd think. The worst they can do is say no.
