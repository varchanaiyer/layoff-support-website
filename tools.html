Prefer to do it yourself? Two prompts to copy

Paste these into any free AI assistant. Check every line it gives you before you use it.

Rewrite resume bullets for a job

Here is a job description and some bullet points from my resume. Rewrite my bullets so they match what this job is asking for. Keep every fact true: don't invent numbers, job titles or skills I didn't mention. Give me three versions of each bullet, from plain to bold.

Job description:
[paste it here]

My bullets:
[paste them here]

Practise an interview out loud

You're a kind but honest interviewer for a [role] job. Ask me one question at a time and wait for my answer. After each answer, tell me one thing that worked and one thing to improve. Start with "Tell me about yourself", then ask why I left my last job (I was laid off), then two questions about my experience.

Paying for a tool already? Ask for hardship pricing

Most paid tools don't advertise hardship pricing on their site. Many will quietly say yes if you ask. Copy this, paste it into the support inbox of any tool you're paying for, change the name, and send it.

Subject: Hardship request — recently laid off

Hi — I've been a paying user since [date]. I was laid off [last week / last month] and I'm trying to keep the tools that are actually useful while I look for the next role. Do you have any kind of unemployed, hardship, or pause option? I'd rather ask than cancel. Thank you for considering it. — [Name]

It works more often than you'd think. The worst they can do is say no.
